The question

If you vanished for 30 days, what would stop?

Most solo founders have a quick answer, and it is not a comfortable one.

The follow-up only you send. The quote only you chase. The handover that lives in your head. It works because you make it work, right up until you are ill, on holiday, or just full.

I call that Founder Glue. It is invisible, it feels normal, and it is costing you more than you think. This morning is about finding it, putting a price on it, and fixing the biggest bit.

What you leave with

Four things, written down, on paper

  • 1

    A map

    Your client journey in six stages, each coloured red, amber or green, showing where it breaks when you are not there.

  • 2

    A £ figure

    What the leaks cost you each month and each year. We use the lower, honest number, so it survives a sceptic doing the maths.

  • 3

    An hours figure

    How many hours a week go on jobs only you can do, and which of them could be automated, templated or handed over.

  • 4

    One leak fixed

    Fixed, or half-fixed with the actual message or checklist drafted, plus a 14-day plan and a partner to keep you honest.

Everything lands on one page, your Founder Glue Map. You photograph it and take it home.

That works. You only use it to look things up, and every answer is written in your workbook. A laptop or tablet is just easier for scrolling an inbox.

No, it is in person in Kelso. A small room is part of what makes the pair work and the hot seat useful.
